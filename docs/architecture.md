# ResearchPilot Architecture

ResearchPilot is an agentic AI research assistant that takes a natural-language research question and autonomously moves through planning, evidence gathering, dataset selection, experimentation, evaluation, and final reporting.

## End-to-End Workflow

```text
Research Question
        ↓
1. Research Planning
        ↓
2. Literature Research
        ↓
3. Dataset Discovery
        ↓
4. Dataset Evaluation
        ↓
5. Dataset Inspection
        ↓
6. Dataset Preparation
        ↓
7. Experiment Planning
        ↓
8. Experiment Execution
        ↓
9. Experiment Analysis
        ↓
10. Final Report
