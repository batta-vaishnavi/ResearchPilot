# Problem & Solution Brief

## Project Title

# ResearchPilot — Autonomous AI Research Agent

## 1. Problem Statement

Conducting academic research is a multi-step and time-consuming process.

A student or researcher typically needs to:

- understand and refine a research question
- create a research plan
- search academic literature
- find suitable datasets
- compare different datasets
- inspect and prepare the selected data
- choose appropriate machine-learning experiments
- train and evaluate models
- interpret the results
- prepare a final research report

These activities often require switching between multiple tools and making decisions manually.

Existing AI assistants can generate explanations or answer research questions, but a simple question-answering system does not independently execute the complete research workflow.

The challenge is to build an AI system that can move from a research goal to an experiment-backed research outcome through coordinated multi-step actions.

---

## 2. Proposed Solution

ResearchPilot is an autonomous AI research assistant that transforms a natural-language research question into a structured, experiment-backed research workflow.

The system combines:

- AI-based planning
- academic literature search
- dataset discovery
- autonomous dataset evaluation
- dataset inspection
- data preprocessing
- experiment planning
- machine-learning experimentation
- result evaluation
- AI-driven analysis
- final report generation

Instead of producing only a textual answer, ResearchPilot performs a sequence of decisions and actions and uses the output of earlier stages to guide later stages.
